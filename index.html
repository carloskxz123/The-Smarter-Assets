Lumpy-Cost Pots

A $600 Christmas becomes about $23 a paycheck, not a December crisis.

Birthdays, school trips, car repairs, the holidays. They aren't surprises. They're lumpy costs: bills you know are coming but that land all at once, so one paycheck has to cover them and you end up short.

  1. Pick a cost that shows up once or twice a year, or type your own.
  2. Choose how often you're paid.
  3. See your slice: the small amount to set aside from every paycheck, so the money is already waiting when the bill arrives.

Turn a big cost into a small one

$23.08per paycheck

Without a plan, the Christmas bill takes $600 out of one paycheck. With a pot, you set aside $23.08 from each of your 26 paychecks, and the money is ready when it arrives.

In the kit: the Lumpy-Cost Pots tab does this for every yearly cost your family has and adds them into one number per paycheck. The Family Cost Finder helps you spot the costs you'd forget.

How it solves the problem

Five moves that keep your account from dipping before payday.

  1. 1

    Give every dollar a job

    Keep it alive

    Weekly caps and a 15-minute check stop the plan from being abandoned in week two.

Questions

Before you download

What exactly do I get?

Three files (PDF and XLSX, 2.4 MB in total): the step-by-step guide and the 11-tab Excel workbook. You can download them right after purchase.

How long does it take to set up?

About three hours for your first plan, which most people split across two evenings. After that, the weekly money check takes about 15 minutes.

I'm not paid every two weeks. Will it still work?

Yes. It works for weekly, every-two-weeks, twice-a-month and monthly pay, and for households where two adults are paid on different schedules.

Do I need to know Excel?

No. You only type in the yellow cells; the white cells calculate by themselves. There are no formulas to build. And if you prefer, the guide works on paper or in any spreadsheet.

What if my partner won't join in?

The guide covers that in its troubleshooting section, along with overtime pay, three-paycheck months and credit card debt, and it includes partner scripts to start the conversation.
